What a Tap Sign is

A table or counter sign with a built-in NFC tag and QR code. Customers tap or scan it with their phone to open a mobile page for your business, built and hosted by tap by techsorted. Depending on your plan, the page can show:

  • Your current specials, and your menu
  • A direct link to leave a Google review
  • Feedback and catering enquiry forms sent straight to you
  • Links to your website and social media

We keep the page running and make your updates for you.

Your page is designed for customers in your premises, opened by tapping or scanning your sign. It isn’t a replacement for your website, so please don’t share the link on social media or elsewhere online, as offers on it are meant for people visiting you.

Specials, prices and offers on your page are notices only. Customers order and pay you directly, and tap by techsorted takes no payments.

Privacy & your data

  • Feedback and catering forms are delivered straight to the emails you’ve nominated above. techsorted does not store, keep copies of, or read these messages; we only count how many are sent.
  • Google reviews are posted on Google under Google’s own terms. We don’t collect or store them.
  • Visitor statistics are anonymous page-view counts with no cookies, used to show you how your sign is performing. No personal information is collected about the people who tap or scan.
  • Your page is hosted on Cloudflare’s global network. Your page content (logo, photos, menu, specials) is held only to run your page.
  • Promotional photos: if you’ve said yes above, we may use photos of your Tap Signs in your premises, with your business name, to promote tap by techsorted. We’ll avoid showing customers or staff, and you can opt out at any time.
  • Your business details and this agreement are kept securely for invoicing and our records (for 7 years after the agreement ends), are never sold or shared, and are handled under the NZ Privacy Act 2020.

Updates

Send changes (specials, menu, prices, details) by email to tap@techsorted.nz. Only your “Specials & Updates Approved Emails” above can update your page; more can be added later. Updates are made within 2–4 hours between 7am and 9pm, 7 days a week. Anything sent after 9pm is done from 7am the next day.

Your plan includes up to 7 specials a month. More can be added on request, which may change your monthly price.
